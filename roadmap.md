# Roadmap

## Done
- Catalogue, categories, prices, photos, stock, contact details and social links all come from the admin dashboard's database.
- Checkout saves real orders (Pending, Unpaid) into the dashboard's Orders screen; staff call to confirm and take payment.
- Contact and quote forms save into the dashboard's Messages screen.

## Waiting on the owner or DataLens
- Branch locations and extra social links are not editable in the dashboard yet.
- Final Privacy and Terms wording (orders are confirmed by phone and paid directly to the shop).
- Delivery charges: confirmed by phone for now.
- Customer order tracking by order number (needs a lookup function in the database).
- Door sizes cannot yet be recorded on an order.
